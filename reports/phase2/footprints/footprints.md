# Footprints

Every assembled part's footprint as used on the boards (`hardware/parts/`), shown as the front-side land pattern (component side, top view) for comparison with each datasheet's recommended land pattern. Coordinates in mm relative to the footprint origin; rotation in degrees. The boards use the bottom-side versions (x mirrored). SVGs show the copper of each footprint.
